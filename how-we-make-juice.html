How We Make Our Premium Juice

Published on March 1, 2026 by David Okello

At Salam Company Ltd, we believe that great juice starts with great ingredients. Our production process is a labor of love, combining traditional harvesting methods with modern food safety standards to bring you the purest tamarind nectar.

1. Wild Harvesting

It all begins in the wild tamarind groves of the West Nile region. Unlike plantation farming, our tamarind is wild-harvested. This means the trees grow naturally without chemical fertilizers or pesticides. Our network of local harvesters hand-picks only the ripest pods, ensuring optimal sweetness and flavor depth.

2. Sorting and Cleaning

Once the harvest arrives at our facility in Arua, it undergoes a rigorous sorting process. We remove any damaged pods or debris. The selected pods are then thoroughly washed and shelled to extract the pulp.

"Quality control is our top priority. We inspect every batch to ensure it meets our high standards before it even reaches the press."

Grace Aboa, Quality Control

3. Cold Extraction

To preserve the delicate vitamins and antioxidants found in tamarind, we use a cold extraction method. We soak and press the pulp at controlled temperatures. This differs from heat-intensive methods that can degrade nutrients and alter the fresh taste of the fruit.

4. Sustainable Filtration

The extracted juice is filtered to remove coarse fibers and seeds. But nothing goes to waste! The leftover pulp and seeds are immediately diverted to our forage production line, where they are processed into high-nutrient animal feed.

5. Pasteurization and Bottling

Finally, the juice is gently pasteurized to ensure it is safe and shelf-stable without the need for artificial preservatives. It is then bottled, sealed, and packed, ready to be delivered to stores and homes across the region.
