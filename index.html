Who We Are

Nature's Goodness in Every Bottle

Founded in 2025, Salam Company Ltd operates at the intersection of beverage production and sustainable waste management. We extract the finest essence of Tamarind for our juices and repurpose the pulp into high-quality porridge.

Our Mission

To create sustainable, income-generating enterprises that use locally available resources to provide decent employment, build practical skills and unlock the creativity and productivity of disadvantaged, Refugee and host community youth.

Our Vision

Self-reliant refugee and host community youth Contributing to inclusive and sustainable economic development

What We Deliver

Beverage Division

Premium Tamarind Nectar

Our signature juice is crafted from hand-picked wild tamarind. Rich in antioxidants and vitamins, it offers a distinct tangy-sweet flavor profile that local markets love. We ensure sustainable processing that retains natural nutrients.

  • 100% Natural Ingredients
  • Locally Sourced Tamarind
  • Hygienic Bottling Process
Sustainability Division

Processed Tamarind porridge

We revolutionize animal feed by processing tamarind pulp and seeds leftover from juice extraction. This zero-waste approach provides essential fiber and energy for livestock, supporting local farmers with affordable feed.
