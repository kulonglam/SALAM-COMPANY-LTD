Our Expertise

From Tree to Table & Trough

We employ advanced processing techniques to ensure maximum nutrition for our juices and zero waste for our porridge production.

1. Tamarind Juice Production

Our juice production line is designed to retain the natural tangy flavor and health benefits of wild tamarind while ensuring international hygiene standards.

1
Wild Harvesting

We source raw tamarind directly from local harvester networks in the West Nile region, ensuring fair pay and organic quality.

2
Cold Extraction

The fruit pulp is extracted using cold-press technology to preserve Vitamin C and antioxidants that heat would destroy.

3
Pasteurization & Bottling

The juice is gently pasteurized to ensure shelf stability without artificial preservatives, then bottled in recyclable packaging.

2. Sustainable porridge Processing

We close the loop on waste. The by-products of our juice line become the raw material for our high-nutrient animal feed.

1
Pulp Recovery

Seeds and fibrous pulp remaining after juice extraction are collected immediately to prevent spoilage.

2
Nutrient Enrichment

The pulp is mixed with millet stalks and other organic binders to create a balanced carbohydrate-protein profile.

3
Drying & Pelletizing

The mixture is solar-dried and compressed into pellets, making it easy to store and digest for livestock.
